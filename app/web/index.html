How a call goes

One call, start to finish.

01

Picks up, day or night

Every call is answered, including the ones at 10:47 pm that used to go unheard. It says it's an AI, in the caller's language.

02

Listens first

The caller describes the project in their own words. Then it asks only what's missing, one question at a time.

03

Checks the fit, in code

Scope, area, timeline and budget are decided by the studio's own rules, not by the AI. Every caller is measured the same way.

04

Books the consultation

It offers two real slots from the right designer's calendar, and confirms only once the booking is actually made.

05

Hands off to a designer

The assigned designer gets the full brief on Telegram within seconds. If nobody acknowledges it, it's re-routed to the studio head.

Asked about cost?

“Pricing depends on the site, the materials you choose, and the scope. Your designer will walk you through it at the free consultation.”

The receptionist never quotes a figure. It doesn't know any, by design.

The rules

Five checks, decided by the studio.

The AI gathers facts. The studio's rules make the call, the same way for everyone.

  1. 01
    Real projectFull design and execution of at least one room. Not advice-only or styling.
  2. 02
    Service areaPune and PCMC, where the studio's vendors are.
  3. 03
    TimelineEnough time for design plus execution. A tight deadline is flagged, not refused.
  4. 04
    Budget, only if offeredIt never asks. If a budget is far below the scope, it says so kindly.
  5. 05
    Decision-makerNoted for the designer. It never declines a caller for this alone.
